# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in the **Salt.Box** community – issues, discussions,
tg chat, and any other project space – to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance or
race and religion.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, experiences, and viewpoints
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them
- Focus on what's best for the community as a whole

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, trolling, or personal/political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all **Salt.Box** project spaces (GitHub, GitLab,
Telegram) and whenever someone is officially representing the project in
public

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **[conduct@saltbox.pro](mailto:conduct@saltbox.pro)**.
All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly, and reporter privacy is
respected
